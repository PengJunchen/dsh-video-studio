# DJI LUT sources / DJI LUT 来源

The bundled restoration LUTs come from DJI's official downloads.
Original pages, download URLs, versions, sizes and SHA-256 hashes are recorded
in [`manifest.json`](manifest.json) and [`library.json`](library.json).
Packaging preserves the original LUT bytes without resampling or rewriting.

随包还原 LUT 来自 DJI 官方下载。原始页面、下载地址、版本、尺寸和 SHA-256
记录在资源清单中；打包保留原始字节，不重采样或改写 LUT。

- [DJI Download Center / DJI 下载中心](https://www.dji.com/downloads)
