# DJI LUT distribution notice

The maintainer has confirmed authorization to include these DJI LUT payloads
in this project's published installation packages. The original LUTs remain
DJI materials; the project's MIT license does **not** relicense them or grant
blanket rights to distribute the LUT files independently.

The exact original download URL, detail page, dimensions, byte size, SHA-256,
and ZIP member/archive identity where applicable are recorded in
[`manifest.json`](manifest.json) and [`library.json`](library.json).
No LUT values are resampled or rewritten during packaging.

Relevant official references:

- [DJI Download Center](https://www.dji.com/downloads)
